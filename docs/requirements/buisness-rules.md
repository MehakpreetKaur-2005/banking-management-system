# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the business rules governing the Banking Management System.

Business rules represent the policies, constraints, validations, and conditions that determine whether a banking operation is permitted.

These rules shall be enforced primarily within the **Business/Service Layer** rather than being implemented only in the Swing user interface.

The business rules defined in this document will provide the foundation for:

* Use Case Specifications
* UML Class Design
* Service Layer Implementation
* Database Constraints
* Exception Handling
* Unit Testing
* Integration Testing
* Requirements Traceability

---

# 2. Business Rule Identification

Each business rule follows the naming convention:

```text
BR-<CATEGORY>-<NUMBER>
```

Examples:

```text
BR-CUST-001
BR-ACC-001
BR-TXN-001
BR-TRF-001
```

Where:

```text
BR       = Business Rule
CATEGORY = Business domain
NUMBER   = Sequential rule number
```

# 4. Employee and Authentication Rules

## BR-AUTH-001 — Employee Authentication

Only registered employees with valid credentials shall be permitted to access protected banking functionality.

---

## BR-AUTH-002 — Employee Account Status

An employee must have an active employee account to successfully authenticate.

An inactive employee shall not be permitted to access the system.

---

## BR-AUTH-003 — Valid Credentials

Authentication shall succeed only when the supplied credentials match the stored employee authentication information.

---

## BR-AUTH-004 — Protected Banking Operations

Customer management, account management, and financial transactions shall require employee authentication.

---

## BR-AUTH-005 — Logout

After logout, the employee shall no longer have access to protected banking operations until authentication is successfully completed again.

---

# 5. Customer Management Rules

## BR-CUST-001 — Unique Customer ID

Every customer shall have a unique customer ID.

Two customers shall not share the same customer ID.

---

## BR-CUST-002 — Customer Identity

Each customer record shall represent one unique banking customer.

---

## BR-CUST-003 — Required Customer Information

A customer shall have all mandatory information populated before registration.

Mandatory fields shall be determined by the final database design.

---

## BR-CUST-004 — Customer Registration

A customer shall be created only after all required validation rules have passed.

---

## BR-CUST-005 — Customer Update

Only valid customer information shall be accepted when updating an existing customer.

---

## BR-CUST-006 — Existing Customer Requirement

A customer must exist in the system before an account can be opened for that customer.

---

## BR-CUST-007 — Customer Deletion

A customer shall not be physically deleted if doing so would violate account or transaction relationships.

Customer records associated with financial history should be retained.

---

# 6. General Account Rules

## BR-ACC-001 — Account Ownership

Every account shall belong to exactly one customer.

---

## BR-ACC-002 — Multiple Accounts

A customer may own multiple accounts.

The system shall not restrict a customer to a single account.

---

## BR-ACC-003 — Unique Account Number

Every account shall have a unique account number.

---

## BR-ACC-004 — Account Number Generation

Account numbers shall be generated by the system rather than manually assigned by an employee.

---

## BR-ACC-005 — Supported Account Types

The system shall support:

```text
SAVINGS
CURRENT
```

Additional account types may be introduced in future versions.

---

## BR-ACC-006 — Account Status

Each account shall maintain a status.

Supported statuses shall initially include:

```text
ACTIVE
CLOSED
```

---

## BR-ACC-007 — Active Account Requirement

Normal banking transactions shall only be performed on active accounts.

---

## BR-ACC-008 — Closed Account Restriction

A closed account shall not be permitted to perform:

* Deposits
* Withdrawals
* Fund transfers

unless a future business rule explicitly permits a specific operation.

---

## BR-ACC-009 — Account Creation

An account shall only be created for an existing customer.

---

## BR-ACC-010 — Account Type Integrity

An account shall have exactly one supported account type.

---

# 7. Savings Account Rules

## BR-SAV-001 — Minimum Balance

A Savings Account shall maintain a minimum balance of:

```text
₹1,000
```

---

## BR-SAV-002 — Savings Withdrawal Rule

A withdrawal from a Savings Account shall be rejected if it causes the balance to fall below ₹1,000.

The rule is:

```text
Balance After Withdrawal >= ₹1,000
```

---

## BR-SAV-003 — Savings Transfer Rule

A transfer from a Savings Account shall follow the same minimum balance restriction as a withdrawal.

The remaining balance after the transfer shall not be less than:

```text
₹1,000
```

---

## BR-SAV-004 — Savings Deposit

A valid deposit may increase the Savings Account balance without violating the minimum balance rule.

---

## BR-SAV-005 — Savings Interest

If interest calculation is implemented, Savings Accounts may be eligible for interest according to the configured interest rate and calculation policy.

---

# 8. Current Account Rules

## BR-CUR-001 — Current Account Overdraft

A Current Account shall permit an overdraft up to:

```text
₹5,000
```

Therefore, the minimum permitted balance shall be:

```text
-₹5,000
```

---

## BR-CUR-002 — Current Account Withdrawal

A withdrawal shall be rejected if it causes the account balance to fall below:

```text
-₹5,000
```

The rule is:

```text
Balance After Withdrawal >= -₹5,000
```

---

## BR-CUR-003 — Current Account Transfer

A transfer from a Current Account shall follow the same overdraft restriction.

The resulting balance shall not be less than:

```text
-₹5,000
```

---

## BR-CUR-004 — Current Account Deposit

A valid deposit shall increase the Current Account balance.

---

# 9. General Monetary Rules

## BR-MONEY-001 — Positive Amount

All monetary transaction amounts shall be greater than zero.

```text
Amount > 0
```

---

## BR-MONEY-002 — Zero Amount

A transaction amount of zero shall not be accepted.

---

## BR-MONEY-003 — Negative Amount

Negative transaction amounts shall not be accepted.

---

## BR-MONEY-004 — Monetary Precision

Monetary calculations shall use an appropriate representation for financial values.

The Java implementation should use:

```java
BigDecimal
```

rather than `double` or `float` for monetary calculations.

---

## BR-MONEY-005 — Currency

The initial system shall operate using:

```text
INR (₹)
```

---

# 10. Deposit Rules

## BR-DEP-001 — Active Account

A deposit may only be performed on an active account.

---

## BR-DEP-002 — Existing Account

The target account must exist before a deposit can be processed.

---

## BR-DEP-003 — Positive Deposit

The deposit amount must satisfy:

```text
Amount > 0
```

---

## BR-DEP-004 — Deposit Balance Calculation

A successful deposit shall calculate the new balance as:

```text
New Balance = Previous Balance + Deposit Amount
```

---

## BR-DEP-005 — Deposit Transaction

A successful deposit shall create a transaction record.

---

## BR-DEP-006 — Deposit Transaction Status

A successfully completed deposit shall be recorded with:

```text
SUCCESS
```

---

## BR-DEP-007 — Failed Deposit

If a deposit cannot be completed, the account balance shall not be incorrectly modified.

---

# 11. Withdrawal Rules

## BR-WD-001 — Active Account

A withdrawal may only be performed on an active account.

---

## BR-WD-002 — Existing Account

The account must exist before a withdrawal can be processed.

---

## BR-WD-003 — Positive Withdrawal

The withdrawal amount must satisfy:

```text
Amount > 0
```

---

## BR-WD-004 — Savings Balance Restriction

For Savings Accounts:

```text
Balance - Withdrawal Amount >= ₹1,000
```

---

## BR-WD-005 — Current Account Overdraft Restriction

For Current Accounts:

```text
Balance - Withdrawal Amount >= -₹5,000
```

---

## BR-WD-006 — Withdrawal Balance Calculation

A successful withdrawal shall calculate:

```text
New Balance = Previous Balance - Withdrawal Amount
```

---

## BR-WD-007 — Withdrawal Transaction

A successful withdrawal shall create a transaction record.

---

## BR-WD-008 — Failed Withdrawal

If a withdrawal violates a business rule, the account balance shall remain unchanged.

---

## BR-WD-009 — Failed Transaction Recording

Where transaction logging is required, a failed withdrawal shall be recorded with:

```text
FAILED
```

and an appropriate failure reason.

---

# 12. Fund Transfer Rules

## BR-TRF-001 — Existing Source Account

The source account must exist.

---

## BR-TRF-002 — Existing Destination Account

The destination account must exist.

---

## BR-TRF-003 — Active Source Account

The source account must be active.

---

## BR-TRF-004 — Active Destination Account

The destination account must be active.

---

## BR-TRF-005 — Different Accounts

The source and destination accounts must be different.

The following operation shall be rejected:

```text
Source Account = Destination Account
```

---

## BR-TRF-006 — Positive Transfer Amount

The transfer amount must satisfy:

```text
Amount > 0
```

---

## BR-TRF-007 — Source Account Balance Rule

The source account must satisfy its applicable balance restriction after the transfer.

For Savings:

```text
Source Balance - Transfer Amount >= ₹1,000
```

For Current:

```text
Source Balance - Transfer Amount >= -₹5,000
```

---

## BR-TRF-008 — Destination Balance

The destination account shall receive the complete transfer amount when the transfer succeeds.

---

## BR-TRF-009 — Atomic Transfer

A fund transfer shall be treated as a single logical operation.

The following operations shall succeed or fail together:

```text
Source Account Debit
        +
Destination Account Credit
        +
Transaction Record Creation
```

---

## BR-TRF-010 — Transfer Commit

The database transaction shall be committed only after all required transfer operations succeed.

---

## BR-TRF-011 — Transfer Rollback

If any required transfer operation fails, the database transaction shall be rolled back.

---

## BR-TRF-012 — No Partial Transfer

The system shall never permanently leave a state where:

```text
Source Account = Debited
Destination Account = Not Credited
```

because of a failed transfer.

---

## BR-TRF-013 — Transfer Transaction Record

A successful transfer shall create the required transaction record(s).

---

## BR-TRF-014 — Transfer Status

A successful transfer shall be recorded with:

```text
SUCCESS
```

A failed transfer shall be recorded as:

```text
FAILED
```

where the transaction logging policy requires failed operations to be retained.

---

# 13. Transaction Rules

## BR-TXN-001 — Unique Transaction ID

Every transaction shall have a unique transaction ID.

---

## BR-TXN-002 — Transaction Type

Every transaction shall have a defined transaction type.

Supported types shall include:

```text
DEPOSIT
WITHDRAWAL
TRANSFER
```

Optional:

```text
INTEREST
```

---

## BR-TXN-003 — Transaction Amount

Every monetary transaction shall have a valid positive amount.

---

## BR-TXN-004 — Transaction Status

Every recorded transaction shall have a valid status.

Initially supported statuses:

```text
SUCCESS
FAILED
```

---

## BR-TXN-005 — Transaction Timestamp

Every transaction record shall contain a timestamp.

---

## BR-TXN-006 — Transaction Account Association

Every transaction shall be associated with the relevant account or accounts.

---

## BR-TXN-007 — Transaction History Preservation

Transaction history shall be retained for completed transactions.

---

## BR-TXN-008 — Transaction Immutability

Completed transaction records should not be directly modified to change the historical financial event.

Corrections, if required, should be represented using appropriate compensating transactions rather than rewriting history.

---

# 14. Account Closure Rules

## BR-CLOSE-001 — Active Account

Only an active account may be closed.

---

## BR-CLOSE-002 — Closure Confirmation

The system shall require employee confirmation before closing an account.

---

## BR-CLOSE-003 — Account Status

After successful closure:

```text
Account Status = CLOSED
```

---

## BR-CLOSE-004 — Closed Account Transactions

A closed account shall not be used for normal banking transactions.

---

## BR-CLOSE-005 — Historical Data

Closing an account shall not delete its historical transaction records.

---

## BR-CLOSE-006 — Account Record Retention

The account record shall remain in the database after closure.

---

# 15. Interest Rules

> **Status: Optional Feature**

## BR-INT-001 — Interest Eligibility

Only eligible account types shall receive interest.

---

## BR-INT-002 — Interest Rate

The applicable interest rate shall be defined by the system configuration.

---

## BR-INT-003 — Interest Calculation

Interest shall be calculated using the formula defined during detailed system design.

For example, a simple-interest implementation may use:

```text
Interest = Principal × Rate × Time
```

The final formula shall be documented before implementation.

---

## BR-INT-004 — Interest Transaction

Applied interest shall generate an appropriate transaction record.

---

## BR-INT-005 — Interest Balance Update

Successfully applied interest shall increase the account balance by the calculated interest amount.

---

# 16. Customer-Account Relationship Rules

## BR-REL-001 — Customer Ownership

Every account must reference an existing customer.

---

## BR-REL-002 — Multiple Account Ownership

A customer may have:

```text
0..N accounts
```

---

## BR-REL-003 — Account Ownership Integrity

An account shall not reference a customer that does not exist.

---

## BR-REL-004 — Customer History

Customer records should remain available when they have associated financial history.

---

# 17. Data Integrity Rules

## BR-DATA-001 — Customer ID Uniqueness

Customer IDs shall be unique.

---

## BR-DATA-002 — Account Number Uniqueness

Account numbers shall be unique.

---

## BR-DATA-003 — Transaction ID Uniqueness

Transaction IDs shall be unique.

---

## BR-DATA-004 — Employee Username Uniqueness

Employee usernames shall be unique.

---

## BR-DATA-005 — Referential Integrity

Foreign-key relationships shall prevent invalid references between:

```text
Customer → Account
Account → Transaction
```

---

## BR-DATA-006 — Required Data

Required entity fields shall not contain `NULL` values.

---

## BR-DATA-007 — Valid Account Status

Only defined account statuses shall be stored.

---

## BR-DATA-008 — Valid Transaction Status

Only defined transaction statuses shall be stored.

---

# 18. Concurrency Rules

## BR-CON-001 — Concurrent Access

The system shall support multiple transaction-processing threads for demonstrating concurrent transaction behavior.

---

## BR-CON-002 — Balance Consistency

Concurrent transactions shall not produce an incorrect final account balance.

---

## BR-CON-003 — No Lost Updates

A successful transaction shall not silently overwrite another successfully committed transaction's balance update.

---

## BR-CON-004 — Transaction Isolation

The database shall use an appropriate transaction isolation mechanism for operations where concurrent access could affect account consistency.

---

## BR-CON-005 — Atomic Balance Update

Balance changes associated with a transaction shall be performed as part of the appropriate database transaction.

---

# 19. Exception Rules

## BR-EXC-001 — Invalid Customer

Operations requiring a customer shall fail when the specified customer does not exist.

---

## BR-EXC-002 — Invalid Account

Operations requiring an account shall fail when the specified account does not exist.

---

## BR-EXC-003 — Closed Account

Banking operations on closed accounts shall be rejected.

---

## BR-EXC-004 — Invalid Amount

Transactions with zero, negative, or otherwise invalid amounts shall be rejected.

---

## BR-EXC-005 — Insufficient Available Balance

A transaction that violates the applicable account balance restriction shall be rejected.

---

## BR-EXC-006 — Duplicate Identifier

Attempting to create an entity with an identifier that must be unique shall result in a validation or persistence error.

---

## BR-EXC-007 — Database Failure

A database failure shall cause the relevant operation to fail safely without committing an invalid partial state.

---

# 20. Audit Rules

## BR-AUD-001 — Transaction Traceability

Every successful banking transaction shall be identifiable using a unique transaction ID.

---

## BR-AUD-002 — Transaction Timestamp

Every transaction shall contain the date and time of processing.

---

## BR-AUD-003 — Employee Traceability

Where supported by the final schema, banking operations shall record the employee responsible for the operation.

---

## BR-AUD-004 — Transaction History

Transaction records shall provide sufficient information to reconstruct the basic history of account activity.

# 24. Fund Transfer Decision Flow

A transfer shall follow the following business flow:

```text
Start
  │
  ▼
Validate Source Account
  │
  ├── Invalid ──→ Reject Transfer
  │
  ▼
Validate Destination Account
  │
  ├── Invalid ──→ Reject Transfer
  │
  ▼
Check Source ≠ Destination
  │
  ├── Same ──→ Reject Transfer
  │
  ▼
Validate Source Account Status
  │
  ├── Closed ──→ Reject Transfer
  │
  ▼
Validate Destination Account Status
  │
  ├── Closed ──→ Reject Transfer
  │
  ▼
Validate Transfer Amount
  │
  ├── Invalid ──→ Reject Transfer
  │
  ▼
Check Balance Restriction
  │
  ├── Violated ──→ Reject Transfer
  │
  ▼
Begin Database Transaction
  │
  ▼
Debit Source Account
  │
  ▼
Credit Destination Account
  │
  ▼
Create Transaction Record
  │
  ├── Failure ──→ ROLLBACK
  │
  ▼
COMMIT
  │
  ▼
Transfer Successful
```

# 25. Business Rules and Java Architecture

Business rules shall primarily be implemented in the Business/Service Layer.

The expected architecture is:

```text
Swing UI
   │
   ▼
Service Layer
   │
   ├── CustomerService
   ├── AccountService
   ├── DepositService
   ├── WithdrawalService
   └── TransferService
   │
   ▼
DAO Layer
   │
   ├── CustomerDAO
   ├── AccountDAO
   └── TransactionDAO
   │
   ▼
MySQL
```

The UI shall collect input and display results, but critical business rules shall not depend solely on GUI validation.

For example:

```text
UI Validation
      ↓
Service Validation
      ↓
DAO
      ↓
Database
```

This ensures that business rules remain enforceable even if another interface is introduced later.

# 26. Business Rules and Object-Oriented Design

The business rules shall influence the object model.

A possible account hierarchy is:

```text
                 Account
                    │
          ┌─────────┴─────────┐
          │                   │
    SavingsAccount       CurrentAccount
          │                   │
   ₹1,000 minimum       ₹5,000 overdraft
```

Common account behavior should be represented in the parent abstraction.

Account-specific rules should be implemented through overriding and polymorphism where appropriate.

```text
BR-TRF-012
```

# 32. Final Business Rule Summary

The Banking Management System shall enforce the following core banking rules:

```text
Customer ID must be unique
        ↓
Customer must exist before account creation
        ↓
Customer can own multiple accounts
        ↓
Account number must be unique
        ↓
Only supported account types are allowed
        ↓
Only active accounts can perform normal transactions
        ↓
Transaction amounts must be greater than zero
        ↓
Savings Account minimum balance = ₹1,000
        ↓
Current Account overdraft limit = ₹5,000
        ↓
Transfers require different valid accounts
        ↓
Transfers must be atomic
        ↓
Failed transfers must roll back
        ↓
Every transaction requires a unique ID
        ↓
Transaction history must be preserved
```

These rules form the core business logic of the Banking Management System.
